Lemon Squeezy provides your license key in the receipt email and My Orders. Copy it there, keep it private, and activate it inside SpeakScribe.

Use SpeakScribe 2.13.0 or newer. To subscribe, open Upgrade or Pro in the extension and choose Monthly or Yearly. Already purchased? Follow the steps below. This website provides instructions; your license is activated inside the extension.
This is the normal activation page. Open your Lemon Squeezy receipt email or visit Lemon Squeezy My Orders with the email address used at checkout, then copy the license key from there. A separately reviewed private support flow may prefill the key in this page; ordinary receipt links do not.
1

Open SpeakScribe

Click the SpeakScribe icon in Chrome, then open the Upgrade or Pro screen. If you have not installed it yet, get SpeakScribe from the Chrome Web Store.

2

Paste the license key

Find "Already have a license key?", paste the key, and click Activate. Your Pro license supports up to three activations.

3

Keep the receipt

The same key can be recovered from your Lemon Squeezy receipt or My Orders page.

Use Lemon Squeezy My Orders to retrieve private purchase details. For billing or activation recovery, email hello@caelicode.com with only a brief request. Do not include a license key, order number, receipt, checkout email, recovery code, or transcript in the initial email; support will provide an authenticated one-time channel. For a general reproducible bug, open a public SpeakScribe support issue. Never include a license key, order number, receipt, checkout email, recovery code, or transcript in a public issue.
