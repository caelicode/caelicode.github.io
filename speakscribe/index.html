Speak. Review.
Carry on.

No new editor to learn. Just a small voice workspace, right where you need it.

  1. 01

    Choose your mode.

    Open SpeakScribe and pick Standard or Local Whisper. Select the field you want to write in.

    Give it a voice.

    Start with the popup, on-page widget or keyboard shortcut. Speak naturally, then stop when you’re done.

  3. 03

    Make it yours.

    Review your words. Insert them into the active field, copy them, or export a transcript to keep.
